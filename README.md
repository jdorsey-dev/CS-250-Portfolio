# CS-250-Portfolio
Portfolio artifact and reflection from CS 250: Software Development Lifecycle
This repository contains my Sprint Review and Retrospective from CS 250. The course focused on Agile and Scrum practices, including user stories, team roles, communication, and adapting to changing requirements throughout the software development process.

## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by first understanding what the user is trying to accomplish and then breaking those needs down into specific features or requirements. User stories help with this because they describe a feature from the user's point of view. This makes it easier to understand why a feature is needed and what the program should accomplish for the user.

## How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach developing programs by breaking larger problems into smaller tasks and working through them step by step. Agile development showed me the importance of being able to adjust when requirements change instead of assuming the original plan will always stay the same. In future development work, I would like to use user stories, backlogs, short development iterations, regular testing, and reviews of completed work.

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member in software development means communicating clearly, completing the work you are responsible for, and keeping the team informed when problems come up. It also means being open to feedback and willing to adapt when requirements or priorities change. Good teamwork is important because software development usually depends on multiple people working together toward the same goal.
